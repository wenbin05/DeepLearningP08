# Interpretation review notes

The files pass structural checks. These notes do not change or discard any ratings
and do not establish how the responses were produced.

## Examples to clarify

- **P04 / S03** in [p04.csv](responses/p04.csv): the explanation contrasts
  “Laura Michelle Mayeaux” with “49-year-old Laura Michelle Mayeaux” and also cites
  omitted incident timing and road-closure duration. The name alone does not
  contradict the age-qualified name, and omission alone is not necessarily a
  factual inconsistency. Clarify which actual claim is considered unsupported.
- **P09 / S03** in [p09.csv](responses/p09.csv): the explanation labels the summary
  inconsistent because it omits a detail about intoxication. Under the supplied
  rubric, omission is not automatically a factual error unless it changes the
  meaning of a claim.

These are examples, not an exhaustive assessment. Differences between respondents
may reflect different interpretations of the rubric. Preserve the original
responses and request clarification rather than rewriting their judgments.

## Reporting boundary

The project team has now confirmed independent reading and comparison of the
articles and summaries by people completing the ratings. Record this as the
team-reported collection method, not as something established by CSV validation.
All responses are retained as submitted. The rubric issues above remain limitations;
no participant's judgment has been rewritten to improve agreement.
